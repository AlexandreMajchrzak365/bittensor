Today's subnets

128 subnets were active in October 2026. Here are ten that show how varied the network is, from raw compute to the weather.

Filter the subnets

  • 64

    Chutes SN64

    Compute

    Runs open AI models on demand through an API: you pay as you go, with no server to manage. One of the largest subnets, and one of the first to live on real revenue.

    Official website · On taostats

  • 4

    Targon SN4

    Compute

    Confidential compute by Manifold Labs: virtual machines are encrypted, so even the owner of the graphics card cannot see what runs on it. Partnership with Intel.

    Official website · On taostats

  • 51

    Lium SN51

    Compute

    Graphics card rental between individuals and companies. About 1.3 million dollars of revenue in one month in 2026, according to its own figures.

    Official website · On taostats

  • 62

    Ridges SN62

    AI models

    AI agents that fix code on their own, in constant competition. The team reports high scores on the SWE-bench test, not yet independently verified.

    Official website · On taostats

  • 56

    Gradients SN56

    AI models

    Train or fine-tune an AI model without writing code: miners compete for the best result.

    Official website · On taostats

  • 120

    Affine SN120

    AI models

    Improves models through reinforcement learning; launched a public AI video studio in October 2026.

    On taostats

  • 9

    IOTA SN9

    AI models

    Trains a large language model spread over the miners' machines, run by the Macrocosmos team.

    On taostats

  • 18

    Zeus SN18

    Other uses

    Weather forecasts based on the European ERA5 climate data.

    Official website · On taostats

  • 44

    Score SN44

    Other uses

    On taostats

  • 8

    Vanta SN8

    Other uses

    On taostats

This world changes very fast: rankings, numbers and even the existence of subnets change from month to month. For today's state, the taostats.io explorer lists every subnet with live figures.

How to judge a subnet?

  • Does it have a product that real customers pay for, outside Bittensor?
  • Is its code public, and is its team identifiable?
  • How much TAO flows in and out? The explorer shows it.
  • Are the announced figures verified by someone other than the team?
