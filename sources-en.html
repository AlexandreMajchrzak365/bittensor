This website's dark pattern

Like every project in the class, this website contains a single, deliberate dark pattern. Did you spot it?

Show the answer

On the Take part page, the “Limited offer!” banner shows a countdown to “book your miner slot”. It is false urgency: there is nothing to book, and the timer restarts from 10 minutes at every visit. This trick pushes people to act without thinking; it is also the favourite weapon of crypto scams, hence the sixth safety rule.
